SBPAS: Smart Business Profit and Analytical System

My main project · C++ console application

SBPAS takes the products of a small business and tells the owner how the business is doing. You enter each product's name, selling price, buying price and quantity, plus the business expenses. The program calculates the results and gives advice on what to improve.

Financial analysis
Total revenue, total cost, gross profit, net profit and net profit margin.
Product analysis
Profit and margin of every product, plus the highest and lowest profit, the highest and lowest margin and the highest revenue.
Statistical analysis
Average profit, profit range, average prices, variance, standard deviation and median profit.
Business decisions
Sorts each product into strong, low or mixed performer, names the best performer and the biggest concern, and gives a final recommendation.
Concepts used
struct, vector, functions, loops, switch menu, input validation, std::sort, sqrt.

I am building SBPAS in stages. Stages 1 to 4 are finished: business calculations, product analysis, statistics and decision support. Stage 5 is in progress: splitting the program into more functions, with better validation and menus. Saving and loading data comes after that.

char getUserChoice(){

char getComputerChoice(){
    srand(time(0));
    int num = rand() % 3 + 1;

    switch(num){
        case 1: return 'r';
        case 2: return 'p';
        case 3: return 's';
    }
    return 0;
}
